StudyQuestion AI ("the app") is an educational question-practice app. This page explains what information the app uses and how.

No account required

You can use the app fully without creating an account or signing in.

What's stored on your device

The following is stored locally on your device only, and is never uploaded to our servers:

  • Recent topics and generated question sets (History)
  • Questions you've bookmarked (Saved)
  • Quiz scores and progress
  • App preferences (default language, board, class, theme)

Clearing your browser/app data, or using "Clear History" in Settings, removes this information.

What's sent to generate questions

When you tap "Generate Questions," the board, class, subject, topic, language, difficulty, question type and count you selected are sent to a third-party AI service in order to generate practice questions. We do not send your name, contacts, device identifiers, camera, microphone or location data — the app does not request access to these.

We avoid retaining the full content of your requests on our backend beyond what's needed for short-term operation, error handling and abuse prevention.

AI-generated content

Questions, answers and explanations are generated with the help of AI and may occasionally contain mistakes. Always verify important academic information with a teacher or official study material.

Children's privacy

The app may be used by school-age students. It does not require login, does not show advertising, does not collect advertising identifiers, and does not request personal information such as an address, phone number, contacts, camera, microphone or precise location.

Reporting a problem

Every generated question has a "Report" option for incorrect answers, unclear wording, inappropriate content or duplicates. You can also reach us using the contact details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
